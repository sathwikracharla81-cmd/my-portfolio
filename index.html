Who I Am

The Story Behind
the Journey

✦

My name is Sathwik Racharla, and I come from Vemulawada, a small town in Telangana, India. My story is not extraordinary in the conventional sense — it is simply one of consistent effort and genuine curiosity.

I studied at a government school right through Class 10, graduating with a 9.7 CGPA. That environment taught me resourcefulness and the value of working hard without many shortcuts. In Class 12, I secured 978 out of 1000 marks and cleared the JEE with a percentile of 85.978 — milestones that opened the door to my next chapter.

Today, I am studying at Newton School of Technology, where I am discovering what it truly means to build things with code. I am drawn to backend systems, logic, problem-solving, and the architecture of how applications work behind the scenes.

One of my most meaningful experiences so far has been competing at a Hacktoberfest hackathon with my team, where we built AMENDMENT — a board game that rewrites its own rules — and won first prize of ₹12,000. That project pushed me to think beyond tutorials and apply what I had learned in a creative, high-pressure setting.

I am still at the beginning of this journey. But I believe beginnings are the most exciting part.

AMENDMENT

The Board Game That Rewrites Its Own Rules

AMENDMENT is a board game where the rules can evolve through controlled amendments. A local AI-powered Court helps interpret and validate proposed rule changes, while a deterministic backend game engine executes approved rules — ensuring fairness, consistency, and an ever-changing gameplay experience.

My Contribution

  • ⚙️ Designed and built the backend architecture for the game
  • 🎲 Engineered the deterministic game engine that processes and enforces rules
  • 🔄 Implemented game-state management and turn-flow logic
  • 🔗 Integrated rule validation and execution pipeline with the AI Court layer
